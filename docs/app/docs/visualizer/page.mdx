# Audio Spectrum Visualizer

OBSMV includes a high-performance audio frequency visualizer designed to react smoothly to game audio, background music, or microphone inputs.

---

## Signal Processing & FFT Engine

The visualizer captures raw audio samples directly from the OBS audio subsystem and applies Fast Fourier Transform processing:

- **Sampling Rate**: Matches the OBS output rate (44.1 kHz or 48 kHz).
- **FFT Size**: 2048 points with Hann windowing for accurate low-frequency resolution.
- **Logarithmic Frequency Mapping**: Frequencies are binned along a perceptual logarithmic scale (20 Hz to 20,000 Hz) so bass, midtones, and treble receive balanced representation.

---

## Visualization Styles

### Vertical Bars (`Bars`)
- Displays distinct vertical spectrum columns.
- **Bar Count**: Adjustable from 8 to 128 bars.
- **Bar Gap**: Pixel spacing between adjacent bars.
- **Bar Corner Radius**: Optional rounding on column top edges.

### Organic Spline Wave (`Wave`)
- Interpolates FFT frequency bands into a continuous, smooth Catmull-Rom spline curve.
- Avoids harsh polygonal angles or jagged vertices.
- Fills the area beneath the spline curve with a smooth vertical color gradient.
- When placed above or below the Now Playing card with `LayoutGap = 0`, the wave connects flush against the card boundary.

### Luminous Crest Line
- An optional high-visibility contour line tracing the exact top ridge of the wave or bars.
- Adjustable thickness and color opacity.

---

## Motion Smoothing & Physics

### Gravity Decay Rate
Controls how quickly frequency peaks fall back toward zero after an audio transient:
- Lower values produce longer sustain (floating peaks).
- Higher values produce rapid, punchy decay.

### Sensitivity Boost
A non-linear gain multiplier that boosts quiet tracks or compresses louder sections to keep the visualizer active across various audio volumes.

### Inertia Filter
An exponential moving average filter that eliminates audio jitter without introducing perceptible latency.
